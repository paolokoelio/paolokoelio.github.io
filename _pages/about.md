<!-- GRC, education and research in info-sec & AI -->
I am a Security Risk Officer in the Global Information Security team at [DLL](https://www.dllgroup.com), where I work on threat-based cyber security risk management across the organization. This includes enterprise-level risk assessments and threat modeling, monitoring the organization's security risk posture, and turning adversary behavior into risks and priorities that both technical and business stakeholders can act on. I work closely with Information Security Officers and subject matter experts across regions, corporate functions and enterprise platforms.

I am also associated with [ICTI](https://ictinstitute.nl/team/pavlo-burda/) for freelance education and research projects. Previously, I worked there as an IT consultant and researcher, advising organizations on information security and ISO 27001 certification, and contributing to educational and research projects, such as [teaching at VU](https://paolokoelio.github.io/teaching/) and sporadic [publications](https://paolokoelio.github.io/publications/). Read more [here](https://paolokoelio.github.io/about_icti/).

I have completed my [PhD](https://paolokoelio.github.io/publication/burda_let_2024) at the <a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/research/research-groups/computer-science/security" target="_blank">SEC Group</a>

of Eindhoven University of Technology where I investigated the interaction between social engineering attacks and human cognition. Selected publications relate to understanding how to study SE attacks [[1](https://paolokoelio.github.io/publication/burda_cognition_2023)] and how to defend against them [[2](https://paolokoelio.github.io/publication/burda_dont_2020), [3](https://paolokoelio.github.io//publication/burda_phishing_2025), [4](https://paolokoelio.github.io/publication/burda_decision-support_2022)]. Read more [here](https://paolokoelio.github.io/about_tue/).

Before joining TU/e, I was a security analyst at a large Italian SOC and loosely involved in personal security projects. I am generally interested in information security and the consequences of [enshittification](https://pluralistic.net/tag/enshittification/) of the Internet.
